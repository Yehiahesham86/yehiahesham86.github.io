01

Opening & Service Evaluation

Confirm identity, set a low-pressure tone, and move into the satisfaction check. Referral and examples are never mentioned here — in Saudi, an unprompted evaluation call is a familiar, trusted format (STC and Zain both call customers this way), so lead with that familiarity, not with a pitch.

Opening line

Hello, this is Rania, a Service Evaluation Specialist at 51Talk. Am I speaking with Umm Faisal, parent of Faisal? I'm calling today simply to check in on your child's recent learning experience — it will only take a couple of minutes.

If the parent seems rushed

I understand you're busy — this isn't a sales call, it's a short service check we do for active families. I just have three quick questions and then I'll let you go.

Example call — routine openingRecorded 04:12
R
Rania · AgentHello, this is Rania from 51Talk's Service Evaluation team — am I speaking with Umm Faisal?

Umm Faisal · ParentAh, okay — yes, go ahead.

Coaching note: naming a familiar local reference point (STC/Zain-style service calls) is what gets the tone accepted in the first 10 seconds.

02

Satisfaction Assessment Probes

Five dimensions build the standard call output: satisfaction level, main feedback, whether there's an issue, whether referral is appropriate, and whether follow-up is needed. The last question is the branch point.

DimensionProbeListening for
AttendanceHow consistent has attendance been recently?Frequent absences, scheduling conflicts
TeacherHow does your child feel about the current teacher?Rapport, requests to switch
ScheduleDoes the current schedule and level still work well?Overload, mismatch
ResponsivenessWhen you've reached out before, how was the response?Unresolved past tickets
OverallOverall, how would you describe the experience — very satisfied, satisfied, or not satisfied?Direct branch signal

Branch point: a clearly positive answer → Section 3. Any hesitation or complaint → Section 5.

03
Branch A · Satisfied

Referral Trigger Script

Enter only once satisfaction is clearly confirmed and there is no open issue. Sequence: experience confirmation → satisfaction → learning-value reinforcement → natural referral ask → example capture → reward confirmation.

Learning-value reinforcement + ask

I'm really glad to hear Faisal has been having a good experience. Feedback like yours, based on real day-to-day use, genuinely helps other parents decide whether to try us. Do you have any friends or family members who've also been thinking about English lessons for their child?

If a name is given

That's great — could I take their name and number? Our team will reach out personally, mention that you recommended them, and confirm your reward once they register, per the current referral terms.

Example call — referral capturedOutcome: Referral logged
R
Rania · AgentIt sounds like things have been going really well for Faisal — I'm glad to hear that. Feedback like yours actually helps other parents a lot. Is there anyone in your family or among friends who's been considering English lessons?
U
Umm Faisal · ParentActually yes — my sister was just asking me about this last week.
R
Rania · AgentWonderful — could I take her name and number? Our team will reach out, mention it's from you, and I'll make sure your referral reward is confirmed once she registers.
U
Umm Faisal · ParentSure, it's Aisha, 05X-XXX-XXXX.
R
Rania · AgentThank you so much — I've noted Aisha as your referral, and our team will follow up within three days.

Never quote a specific reward figure without confirming the referral terms currently in effect.

Branch A2 · Fallback

Check-in / Share Fallback

Use when the parent is satisfied but has no example on hand. The goal is to secure at least a check-in or share — a call is never a dead end.

That's completely fine — if no one specific comes to mind right now, would you be willing to help us with a quick check-in or share about Faisal's experience instead? It takes less than a minute and helps other parents see genuine feedback.

Branch B · Dissatisfied

Issue Resolution Scripts

The moment dissatisfaction surfaces, stop the evaluation flow and move to resolution. No referral talk in this branch — resolve first, revisit referral potential on a future call.

General acknowledgment

Thank you for letting me know — I want to make sure we get this right for you. Let me look into this and see what I can do directly, or make sure it reaches the right person.

Issue typeScript
Occasional leaveI can process an occasional leave for you now — could you confirm the date(s) and the reason?
Tutoring / extra helpI can arrange a tutoring session to help with that — let me check what's available and get this scheduled.
Schedule adjustmentI can help adjust the schedule — what times would work better going forward?
Teacher changeI can submit a request to review the teacher assignment and flag your feedback for a better fit.
CompensationGiven what you've described, you qualify for one additional lesson as a gesture to make this right.
Example call — schedule complaint, resolved on-callOutcome: Resolved, no referral requested
S
Umm Sara · ParentHonestly, the class time hasn't worked for us in weeks — Sara's been missing half of them.
R
Rania · AgentThank you for telling me — I want to fix this properly rather than just note it. I can help adjust the schedule now. What times would actually work going forward?
S
Umm Sara · ParentEvenings after 7pm would be much better.
R
Rania · AgentI've updated that for you within the available options. We won't talk about referrals today — my priority is making sure this is actually fixed for Sara first.
5.7 · Outside permission scope

Escalation Script

This is something I want to make sure gets the right attention, so I'm escalating it directly to our CC / SS / Customer Service team today. You'll hear back within a few business days, and I'll personally note this so it isn't lost.

Rule: never promise an outcome outside the permissions in Section VI of the project doc (outbound tool, CRM query, referral registration, leave/make-up, class/teacher assistance, escalation mechanism). Escalate rather than guess.

Objections & FAQ

ObjectionResponse
"I don't have anyone in mind."That's fine — would a quick check-in or share work instead? No pressure either way.
"I already referred someone before."Thank you — is there anyone else who's come up since, or would you like me to check that referral's status?
"What do I get out of it?"Referrals that go on to attend and pay are rewarded under our current terms — I can confirm the exact details for your case.
"I'm not comfortable sharing their number."Understood — you're welcome to share our contact with them directly instead.
"Is this a sales call?"No — this is a service check for active families; referral only comes up if you're happy with the experience.
"I don't have time right now."No problem — is there a better time to call back, or should I just note that everything's going well?
07

Closing Scripts

After a satisfied / referral call

Thank you again for your time and your feedback — it genuinely helps us. Have a great day, and we're always here if you need anything.

After an issue-resolution call

Thank you for your patience — to summarize, I've made the adjustment and escalated the remaining item to the right team, who'll follow up soon. Is there anything else I can help with today?

Voicemail / no answer

Hello, this is Rania from 51Talk's Service Evaluation team — I was calling to check in on your child's learning experience. Please call back whenever convenient, or we'll try again shortly.

09

Call Data Logging

  • Call outcome — connected / no answer / call back requested
  • Satisfaction level captured
  • Main feedback (1–2 lines, free text)
  • Service issue identified, and type
  • Issue resolved on-call, escalated, or unresolved
  • Referral triggered — name/contact if provided
  • Check-in / share completed as fallback
  • Follow-up required, and by whom
10

Quick Reference Card

1. Open with service evaluation — never lead with referral.

2. Assess satisfaction across the 5 probe dimensions.

3. Satisfied → referral ask, or check-in/share if no example.

4. Dissatisfied → resolve within permissions, or escalate.

5. Close with the matching script, then log the call.

Golden rule: resolve before you ask — and never end a satisfied call empty-handed.
